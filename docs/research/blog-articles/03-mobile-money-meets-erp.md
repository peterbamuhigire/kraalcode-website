# Article evidence record: mobile money meets ERP

Date checked: 15 August 2026. Research owner: Kraal Code Editorial Team.

## Editorial argument

Mobile-money integration is an evidence and reconciliation workflow, not only a payment callback. The article covers collections, disbursements, provider references, duplicate handling, exception queues, EFRIS boundaries, and control-account review.

## Limits

Endpoint availability, country coverage, commercial credentials, limits, fees, and production support must be confirmed with the provider and customer. The article does not claim that a mobile-money payment replaces a fiscal document or accounting reconciliation.
