# Research note — Why African Businesses Are Moving Beyond Spreadsheets to Cloud ERP

**Date researched:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** Drafted for editorial review  
**Audience:** East African business owners, finance leaders, operations managers, and NGO administrators  
**Claim scope:** Cloud ERP decision guidance; not a claim that every organisation should replace every spreadsheet

## Argument

Organisations move beyond spreadsheets when shared transactions, approvals, branches, and audit evidence have become more important than the workbook’s low starting cost. Cloud ERP is useful when it creates a controlled shared record, but it does not replace process design, reconciliations, or human review.
