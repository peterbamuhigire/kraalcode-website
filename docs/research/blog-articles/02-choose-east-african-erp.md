# Article evidence record: choosing an East African ERP

Date checked: 15 August 2026. Research owner: Kraal Code Editorial Team.

## Editorial argument

ERP selection should be based on the buyer's real workflows, cloud responsibility model, controls, total cost, pilot evidence, and exit plan. Country-specific tax, e-invoicing, payroll, and statutory requirements remain deployment questions.

## Limits

The article does not assert that one ERP meets the laws of every East African country. Product, integration, support, and pricing statements are buyer-verification prompts, not independent certification.
