# Article evidence record: multi-branch operations

Date checked: 15 August 2026. Research owner: Kraal Code Editorial Team.

## Editorial argument

Branch growth needs explicit location identity, transfer status, approval rights, reconciliation, local detail, and consolidated reporting. The cited vendor documentation is used as a model reference for locations, transfers, planning, and consolidation, not as a claim about Kraal Code's implementation.
